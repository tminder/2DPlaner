Planagonia Is Live: What's New

August 31, 2026

Two days ago, Planagonia was a pile of experiments living only on one computer. Today it's a real website — one you can visit, sign up for, and use to sketch out a floor plan, with your work following you between devices instead of being stuck in a single browser tab. Here's what changed, and why it should matter to you rather than just to us.

You can finally create an account

Until now, whatever you drew in Planagonia only ever lived in the one browser you drew it in — close it on a different computer and your plan simply wasn't there. That's fixed. You can now sign up with just an email address and a password, confirm your email with one click, and save your plans to your account instead of just to your browser. Start a layout on your laptop at home, check it later on your phone — it'll be right there.

We kept the sign-up itself as small as we could: no username to invent, no extra fields. Just an email and a password, and we take care of the rest.

The whole site now actually works on a phone

Until this week, Planagonia was designed with a full-size computer screen in mind — which meant it was genuinely awkward to use on a phone. We went through the entire site and fixed that, without changing how it looks or works on a real computer:

  • The menu at the top of every page now tucks neatly behind a small ☰ icon instead of spilling across the screen.
  • Inside the app itself, the code and the drawing — previously squeezed on top of each other, both too small to actually use on a small screen — are now separate Code and Viewer tabs you can switch between with a tap.
  • The row of buttons along the top of the app is shorter and calmer. The everyday ones stay put; the occasional ones (Rename, Delete, and the rest) now live behind a small "···" button, out of the way until you actually need them.

A few small, honest additions

  • A proper legal notice page, with our real contact details.
  • A short, plain note about what the site actually stores in your browser — your plans and your sign-in state, nothing more, and no tracking or third-party cookies involved.
  • A small "Beta" label on the app itself. It's still evolving quickly, and we'd rather say so than let you find out the hard way.

Squashing what wasn't quite right

Even a young product picks up rough edges fast, and two were worth calling out:

  • Dragging certain shapes around a plan could silently stop working, with nothing on screen telling you why. Found and fixed.
  • The button that recenters your view on your whole plan wasn't reliably doing that — sometimes doing nothing at all, sometimes leaving part of your plan cropped out of view. It now reliably shows your entire plan again, however far you'd zoomed or scrolled around.
Curious what any of this actually looks like? Open the app and try it — no account needed just to look around, though now you can save your work to one if you'd like it to follow you.

We'll keep posting here as Planagonia grows — the next things on our list include letting you resend a confirmation email if the first one gets lost, and jumping straight from a saved plan on your profile into the app. Thanks for reading, and for trying something this new.
