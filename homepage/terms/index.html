Terms & Privacy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Plain language, on purpose. This page describes what Planagonia's accounts and cloud sync actually do today, written by the one person who builds it — not boilerplate copied from somewhere else. It isn't a substitute for professional legal advice, and it will be updated as the service changes.

What this covers

Planagonia itself — the plan editor at /app — works entirely in your browser with no account at all. Everything below only applies if you create an account to sync plans to the cloud.

What we collect

  • To create an account: an email address and a password. The password is used once, to create your account; it never becomes your day-to-day sign-in credential — after you confirm your email, you're shown a separate, generated credential for actually signing in.
  • While signed in: a short-lived session token (expires after 1 hour), kept only in your browser tab and never written to long-term storage.
  • When you explicitly save to the cloud: the text of the plan you saved, under your account. Saving is never automatic — it only happens when you click "Save to Cloud."

That's the complete list. We don't run analytics, we don't use third-party trackers or advertising cookies, and we don't collect anything beyond what's written above.

Where it's stored

Everything lives on infrastructure we run ourselves, hosted in Switzerland — a self-hosted WordPress instance handles account credentials, and a separate small service stores your saved plans. Neither is shared with, or processed by, any third party. Without an account, your plans live only in your own browser's local storage, which we never see at all.

What we do with it

Your account and plan data are used for exactly one thing: letting you sign in and get your own plans back. We don't sell it, share it, or use it to train anything. If that ever changes for a specific, concrete reason, this page will say so before it happens, not after.

Deleting your account or data

Self-service account deletion isn't built yet. Until it is, email info@planagonia.com and we'll remove your account and any plans saved under it.

Using the service

  • Plans you create and save are yours. This doesn't affect the separate license covering Planagonia's own source code (see the LICENSE in the repository) — that's about the app's own code, not what you build with it.
  • Don't use the registration or sign-in system to create accounts in bulk, automate abuse, or otherwise interfere with the service for other users.
  • The service is provided as-is, at an early stage, with no uptime guarantee. We'll do our best to keep it running and to tell you about anything that affects your data.
  • This service isn't intended for anyone under 16.

Changes to this page

If these terms change, we'll update the date at the top. Continuing to use an account after a change means you accept the update.

Contact

Questions, deletion requests, anything else: info@planagonia.com. See the Impressum for who operates this site.
