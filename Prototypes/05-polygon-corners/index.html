2DPlaner prototype: deformable polygon corners

Throwaway prototype extending D-018 (points as node references) from open line segments (see 04-shared-corners) to a closed shape. panel is a polygon whose 4 vertices are each their own corner_* node. It starts as an axis-aligned rectangle, but nothing enforces that — drag any one corner and the shape deforms into a general (non-right-angled) quadrilateral, while the other three corners stay exactly where they are. No new language primitive was needed: this is the same points-reference mechanism as 04, just applied to a closed shape instead of separate wall segments.

Also adds D-020's settings preamble block, with a first concrete attempt at F-004: allowUnrealisticPolygons. "Unrealistic" here means self-intersecting (a "bowtie") — not non-convex, since a concave shape like an L-shaped room is perfectly realistic. Default is false (checked): drag a corner far enough to cross the opposite side and the drag is rejected outright — the shape simply stops following the cursor, with a message explaining why — rather than letting it go self-intersecting and flagging it after the fact. Set it to true to disable the check entirely.

Known simplification: since all 4 of panel's points are corner references (none are literal), there's no literal coordinate left to translate — so you can't drag the panel's body to slide the whole shape, only reshape it by dragging individual corners (dragging the body shows a message explaining why, per corner). Translating a polygon as a whole would need a different mechanism (e.g. an explicit offset applied to all corners) that isn't built here.
