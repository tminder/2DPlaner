2DPlaner prototype: creating connections by dragging

Answers three questions from D-014's kitchen-row test (06): (1) what happens if connected objects aren't adjacent — nothing special, a connection means "moves together", not "touches"; try editing ablage's position to leave a gap, then drag. (2) Connections can now be created by dragging, not just typed in code: hold Ctrl, press down on one element, drag to another, release. (3) "Node-wise" connections: herd_right and ablage_left are small anchor-point nodes (children of herd/ ablage, D-016-style — no shape, invisible until hover) sitting at the meeting edge. Ctrl-drag between two anchors instead of two whole objects to connect at that specific point rather than "these two objects" vaguely.

Nothing is pre-connected — the plan starts blank of connections so you can create every kind yourself: object-to-object (drag from anywhere on herd's body to anywhere on ablage's body), or anchor-to-anchor (from the small dot at herd's right edge to the one at ablage's left edge — hover to find them).

Known limitation worth noticing, not hiding: anchor nodes are positioned as plain children (D-013) with a literal offset from their parent — they render attached to the parent automatically (child coordinates are parent-relative), but a plain drag on the anchor itself only edits its own position, not the parent's. Try connecting the two anchors, then plain-dragging herd_right alone: it detaches visually from herd's edge while dragging ablage_left along with it (the connection). Whether anchors should instead be "handles" that move their whole parent is an open design question this makes concrete, not a bug to silently fix.
