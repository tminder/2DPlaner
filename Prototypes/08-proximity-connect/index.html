2DPlaner prototype: connect by proximity

Narrower than 07, on purpose: plain drag only, no separate gesture, no connecting things that aren't adjacent. herd and ablage start apart, unconnected. Drag herd next to ablage and let go — nothing connects automatically. Instead: click the object to select it (drag-and-release also selects). While selected, a small green + icon appears right at the point where it touches any not-yet-connected neighbor — click it to connect. An existing connection shows a red × icon at the same spot to disconnect. Both icons sit directly at the contact point between the two objects, not in a separate list.

The × icon is the only visible trace of an existing connection — no permanent connector line — and it only renders while one of the two connected objects is selected; click empty space to deselect and hide it again. Hovering either connected object (regardless of selection) highlights both of them in orange, so the relationship is still visible without clicking.

Scope, deliberately: adjacency is checked against whole objects' bounding boxes (not node-wise anchor points like 07 — dropped here to isolate this one question). "Adjacent" means edges touching or within 5cm, not just literally flush to the millimeter. Moving connected objects apart again does not remove the connection automatically — you have to select and click the × icon — that's a deliberate choice now, not just an omission. The contact-point calculation handles side-by-side and stacked adjacency but not corner-only touching.
